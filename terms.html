5. Causeway checkpoints

The checkpoint card shows approach-road speed bands on the Singapore side only — how fast traffic is moving on the BKE and AYE towards Woodlands and Tuas. It is not immigration clearance time, queue time, or waiting time, and no public source publishes those. Speeds are indicative only and can change suddenly with traffic conditions, customs counter operations, and other factors. Do not use this card as a substitute for official checkpoint information, and always check the official feed before travelling.

Do not use this site while driving. Check conditions before you set off, or have a passenger check them.

6. Air quality (PSI) and weather

  • The PSI shown is the 24-hour PSI, a rolling average — not the air quality right now. For current conditions, check the 1-hour PM2.5 reading at haze.gov.sg.
  • Nothing on this site is intended to provide medical advice, or to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any health condition. Any health guidance shown with the PSI is general public guidance only, not personal medical advice. If you have a health condition, follow your doctor's advice and the official NEA / MOH advisories.
  • Weather is as reported by the source at fetch time and can change quickly, especially in the tropics. Forecasts may not occur as predicted. Do not rely on the weather shown here as a substitute for official weather alerts, emergency information, or public safety instructions.
